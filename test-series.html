EVALUATION RUBRICS

How We Evaluate Subjective Mains Copies

A transparent 5-parameter criteria applied to every single subjective answer sheet.

1. Question Comprehension (20%)

Did the student answer the exact directive keyword (Critically Examine, Discuss, Evaluate, Elucidate)? Did they address all sub-parts of the prompt?

2. Conceptual Depth & Linkages (30%)

Depth of arguments, multi-dimensional linkages (economic, environmental, constitutional), and absence of superficial generalizations.

3. Substantiation & Case Laws (20%)

Relevant Supreme Court precedents, Law Commission reports, official committee findings, NITI Aayog data, and international best practices.

4. Structural Organization (15%)

Clear paragraph transitions, prominent underlined sub-headings, logical sequence, and balanced use of schematic diagrams.

5. Conclusion & Way Forward (15%)

Constructive, constitutional, forward-looking policy synthesis rather than pessimistic criticism or abrupt closures.

Personalized Margin Feedback

Mentors write actionable marginal annotations explaining precisely why marks were awarded or deducted on each specific answer.
