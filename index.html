METHODICAL PROGRESSION

The UPSC Preparation Journey

A proven 4-quarter roadmap transforming beginner aspirants into examination-ready civil service candidates.

01

Foundation & NCERTs

Months 1–3: Master fundamental concepts across NCERT books (Classes 6–12), syllabus mapping, and daily newspaper habits.

02

Standard References

Months 4–7: Deep-dive into standard authoritative texts (Polity, Modern History, Economy), alongside starting optional subject studies.

03

Prelims Intensive Drills

Months 8–10: High-frequency test series, CSAT mastery, government schemes, economic survey, and targeted revision cycles.

04

Mains Answer Writing

Months 11–12: Daily timed answer writing, full-length 3-hour tests, GS-IV ethics case studies, and interview guidance orientation.
