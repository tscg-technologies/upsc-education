ELIGIBILITY GUIDANCE

UPSC CSE Eligibility Overview

Before enrolling in civil services preparation, aspirants should verify standard eligibility conditions outlined by the Union Public Service Commission:

Nationality & Educational Qualification

Nationality: Candidate must be a citizen of India for IAS, IPS, and IFS.

Degree: Candidate must hold a degree from any recognized Central, State, or Deemed University, or possess an equivalent qualification. Final year candidates may appear for Prelims provided degree proof is submitted prior to Mains.

Age Limits & Permissible Attempts (Illustrative)

  • General / EWS Category: 21 to 32 years of age | 6 attempts.
  • OBC Category: 21 to 35 years of age | 9 attempts.
  • SC / ST Category: 21 to 37 years of age | Unlimited attempts up to age ceiling.
  • PwBD Candidates: Additional relaxations as per governmental norms.
